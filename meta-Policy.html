Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Alfa provides an automated and AI-assisted messaging service for responding to messages sent to our Instagram account. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

When a user sends a direct message to the Alfa Instagram account, the application may process the following information:

  • Instagram account identifier or username;
  • Message content sent by the user;
  • Message date and time;
  • Technical information provided through the official Meta API.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. Users should not send passwords, banking information, identification documents, medical information, or other confidential data through Instagram Direct Messages.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed only for the following purposes:

  • Receiving messages through the official Meta API;
  • Understanding the user's request;
  • Generating an automated or AI-assisted response;
  • Sending the response back to the user through Instagram;
  • Maintaining the security and proper operation of the service.

Information is not used for behavioral advertising, unrelated marketing, or the sale of personal data.

3. Automated and AI Processing

Messages may be processed automatically using artificial intelligence services in order to generate relevant responses.

Depending on the technical configuration of the service, message content may be transmitted to service providers used for automation, hosting, artificial intelligence processing, or system operation. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Users should avoid sending confidential or highly sensitive information through direct messages.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Alfa retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the messaging service, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, or comply with legal obligations.

Some technical logs or workflow execution records may be retained temporarily by our automation, hosting, or artificial intelligence service providers.

Where technically possible, message information and related logs are deleted or anonymized when they are no longer required.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared only with service providers necessary for operating the application, including:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., for receiving and sending Instagram messages;
  • Artificial intelligence service providers used to generate responses;
  • Automation, hosting, database, or infrastructure providers used to operate the application;
  • Government authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Meta's Privacy Policy is available at: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We will review valid requests and respond within a reasonable period, subject to technical and legal limitations.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not intentionally directed to children under the minimum age required to use Instagram in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this application.
